BLOOM CREATIVES · WEBSITE INFORMATION

Privacy Policy.

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains how BLOOM CREATIVES, operated by Chenitha Kodithuwakku, handles information when you visit this portfolio website, send a project inquiry or submit client feedback.

1. Information you choose to provide

If you use the project inquiry form, the information you submit may include your name, email address, project type, estimated budget and project brief. The form is processed by Web3Forms and delivered to the portfolio owner so your inquiry can be answered.

If you submit a client review, the form collects your name, email address, rating and written feedback. Feedback is stored in a Supabase database. Reviews are held for moderation before publication. Your email address is not displayed with public reviews and is restricted from public read access.

2. Website analytics

This website offers optional Google Analytics. Analytics is not loaded unless you choose “Accept” in the analytics preference notice on the home page; choosing “Reject” keeps analytics disabled. If accepted, Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies and may process technical information such as device, browser and approximate location data. You can clear your browser's site data to reset your choice. For information about Google's processing, see Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Third-party services

  • Web3Forms: processes project inquiry form submissions. See Web3Forms' privacy information.
  • Supabase: stores client feedback and supports the public approved-review display. See Supabase's privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts: supplies website typography. Your browser connects to Google's font servers when fonts are loaded.
  • GitHub Pages / Cloudflare Pages: the website may be delivered through its hosting and deployment providers, which may process technical request logs.

4. How information is used

Information is used to respond to project inquiries, review and moderate submitted feedback, publish approved reviews, maintain the website and understand broad website usage. It is not intentionally sold to third parties.

5. Retention and security

Project inquiry information may remain in the form service and email inbox for as long as needed to handle the request and maintain business records. Feedback submissions may be retained in the database while the portfolio and review feature remain active. Reasonable technical safeguards are used, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Your choices and requests

You can choose not to submit information through the forms. You may request access to, correction of or deletion of information you submitted by emailing bloom.creativesss@gmail.com. Please identify the form submission and the email address used so the request can be located. Some information may need to be retained where required for legitimate business or legal reasons.

7. Children's privacy

This portfolio is intended for general audiences and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. Please contact the email above if you believe a child has submitted personal information.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when website features or service providers change. The latest version and update date will be shown on this page.

Questions about this policy? Contact bloom.creativesss@gmail.com.
